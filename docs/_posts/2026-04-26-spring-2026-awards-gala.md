## Organization Updates

Finally, we discussed any changes to the format of general proceedings and schedule for the Fall 2026 term. Below are the key updates.

- The general meeting time has moved from _Fridays at 3pm_ to _Fridays at 11am_ to better accommodate faculty and members.
- The voting format shall remain the same. That is, a member shall be selected at random each week, where they will propose 3 papers which will be voted on. The winning paper is the subject of the following meeting, and a new member is selected for the following meeting's vote.
- Instead of having a gap week between the 1st week and 3rd week meetings, Faculty Advisor _Yuyan Bao_ proposed a meeting to discuss each member's topic of research. As such, the 2nd week meeting will be an elevator pitch style meeting where members will give a brief (up to 5min) elevator pitch of their active research topics.
- New award categories for the Fall 2026 term will be selected on the 1st meeting of the term. As last time, old categories can be recycled as well as new catergories can be proposed. 6-8 categories will be selected, which will be voted on at that term's Awards Gala.
